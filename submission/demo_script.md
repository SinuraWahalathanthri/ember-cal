# 30-second demonstration
0–5s: Ask, “Is this an unusual fire season, or a different satellite?” Show the overview.
5–12s: Open the Sri Lanka explorer; switch raw MODIS to harmonized. Say explicitly that this is synthetic demonstration data.
12–18s: Select a region or draw an area, then inspect a complete calendar month and the historical rank.
18–23s: Open seasonal and annual comparisons.
23–27s: Download a labelled CSV or PDF briefing.
27–30s: Show accuracy status: validation pending. State next step: independent field and burn-scar validation before an agency pilot.
