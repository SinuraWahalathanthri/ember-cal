# Proposed three-month Sri Lanka agency pilot
This is a proposal, not a claim of pilot readiness or an active agency partnership.
Month 1: obtain authentic historical data, establish agency ownership and independent validation reviewers, and verify land-cover/static-source filtering.
Month 2: calibrate and compare held-out results, publish confidence intervals, and test geometry queries and reference-sensor handover.
Month 3: deploy access controls, audit logging, backups and an approved notification channel; trial alerts in parallel with existing procedures, with no automated dispatch.
Proceed only after documented scientific and operational acceptance by the participating agency. A potential DMC/Forest Department partner must be contacted separately by the project owner.
